# Presentation Project

`presentation.json` is the source of truth. Inspect and reuse `assets/`, keep paths project-relative, and use image frames large enough to read. Preserve IDs for continued concepts and validate after editing. For a staged entrance, use `{ "entrance": <type>, "order": <positive integer> }`; matching orders reveal together. Do not author animation delays or durations, and use reveals sparingly so Morph remains clear.
